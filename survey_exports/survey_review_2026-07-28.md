## Welcome

### Question 0

**The National Emergency Number Association (NENA) is conducting this survey to collect data from Public Safety Answering Points (PSAP) in the United States and Canada to evaluate and update call processing and staffing guidelines in response to evolving operational demands, emerging technologies, and increasing service complexity. The results will identify national trends, challenges, and effective practices to support data-driven decisions and the development of practical modern staffing recommendations that enhance efficiency, sustainability, and service quality. The survey takes approximately 20 to 30 minutes to complete. The deadline for completion is 07/31/2026. This initial phase focuses on core operations such as call processing, dispatching, and frontline Full Time Equivalent (FTE) staffing. Future phases will include support and administrative functions. Broad participation is essential to ensure representative results. All responses will be analyzed in aggregate to support research and guideline development.

***Please coordinate within the center to ensure that there is only one response per PSAP***.

If you have multiple PSAPs under the same governance, please complete a survey for each PSAP to ensure that we can capture the unique characteristics of each center.**
