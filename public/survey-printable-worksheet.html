Welcome & Survey Overview

INFO The National Emergency Number Association (NENA) is conducting this survey to collect data from Public Safety Answering Points (PSAP) in the United States and Canada to evaluate and update call processing and staffing guidelines in response to evolving operational demands, emerging technologies, and increasing service complexity.
Survey Duration: ~20-30 minutes | Deadline: 03/01/2027 | Reference Year: Calendar Year 2025
Key Reminders: • Only one response per PSAP
• Multiple PSAPs under same governance = separate survey each
• Coordinate data gathering across team members before starting
• On submission, you'll get a copy of your responses for your records (browser-generated, never sent to server)

Data Requirements Checklist

Gather the following data before starting the survey:

  • ☐ Census and demographic data for your PSAP's service area
  • ☐ Call volume data for calendar year 2025
  • ☐ Staffing data (telecommunicators and supervisors)
  • ☐ Shift length, count, and staffing per shift
  • ☐ Average phone answer times
  • ☐ Call processing times
  • ☐ Census data available at census.gov
Progress Saving: Use the 'Save & Get Code' button in the online survey to save progress and return later. The code is valid for 7 days from generation.

Data Privacy and Usage

INFO All data collected will be treated with strict confidentiality and used solely for developing staffing guidelines, call processing standards, and industry analytics for PSAPs. Data will be aggregated and anonymized so no specific PSAP can be identified in reports or publications.
